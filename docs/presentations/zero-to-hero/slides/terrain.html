10

The terrain lives in Grid1.cpp as GridNode, DrawableGridNode and DrawableGridNodeSharedTextures. Each GridBaseBlock is 0xd50 bytes holding 17x17 eight-byte samples; arrays are LZW-compressed when smaller. Heights pass through the nonlinear curve shown: at i=0 it's 0, at 255 it's 256 squared minus one. Nodes subdivide by screen-space error across 9 levels, pin vertices to neighbours to avoid T-junction cracks, and free GPU buffers when AgeManager evicts them. GridNode slot 1 casts a segment against the height field; it uses inline asm so it's out of reach for now. Collision uses a separate QuadTree, visibility a VisibilityQuadTree with sphere-in-frustum tests. This is also what Armadillo and Photoshop height maps feed in the modding scene.
