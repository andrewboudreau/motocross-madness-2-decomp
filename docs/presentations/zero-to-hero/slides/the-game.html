Released May 25, 2000 in North America. Designers Robb Rinard and Stephane Roncada (a pro 125 Supercross racer who designed the Supercross tracks); lead programmer Glenn O'Bannon on physics and gameplay; Mark DeSimone as technology lead, who built the eco-system that manages tens of thousands of trees and shrubs by placement rules. Design goal from the interview: get past the "fantasy barrier" to reach racing fans beyond motocross, and extend replay value through Armadillo and multiplayer. Reception: CGW best racing game of 2000, PC Gamer Editor's Choice, AIAS PC Sports award.
