In the 2000 interview, the team said Mark DeSimone's eco-system "manages tens of thousands of trees, bushes, and shrubbery", placed by rules to make forests and deserts. The decomp shows how. EcoSystem derives from GameObject and GroundFogableObject. Species definitions are .est files. Instances come from a placement bitmap, from stored .esb data decoded with a running XOR key, or from an Auto generator that weighs slope and altitude through a tabulated normal distribution and samples the green channel of probability TGAs, using 5x5 or 6x6 rings chosen by an "EcoGen" registry value. The "two pixels" reading of the billboard threshold is my interpretation of radius*scale/depth > 2; the docs give only the formula. Most of the EcoSystem draw and generator code is still a near miss.
