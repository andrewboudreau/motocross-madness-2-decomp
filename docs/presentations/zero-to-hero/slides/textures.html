Textures pass through four stages. Tgafile.cpp understands 34 file-format codes; formats 6–12 and 0x15–0x1b are LZW-compressed, decoded by a variable-width LZW15V decoder with a 35,023-entry dictionary. PixTrans converts between engine pixel formats, downsamples and dithers; a ColorMapper in Quantize.cpp builds 555/565 palette tables. TextureMapManager groups small textures and packs them onto 256x256 CacheTexture pages within the TextureCacheLimit setting, which the race loader sizes from available video memory. The model system is called Soultree: D3DIMSoultreeObject loads .slt/.slb models through the Parameterblocks reader, with LODs and modifier lists like MorphBastardModifier reading RiderMorph.mbf, and GhostMod for ghost riders.
