The honest headline: 66.0 percent of game-code bytes, 827,765 bytes, are strict exact, about 4 percent of that only in samples. Another 28.0 percent is registered but not exact, 3.4 percent needs inline assembly and is excluded under current rules, 2.5 percent is alignment padding, and 0.03 percent has no registration at all. The project is explicit that its 99.7 percent figure, 2,980 of 2,990 selected targets, is "not 99.7% of MCM2": targets are chosen for usefulness and tractability, and the full function inventory isn't established. No game function is yet a complete original translation unit. But all 392 translation units now compile and link into a runnable executable that loads a track and races, with near-miss and unregistered bodies standing in as best-effort code. So it plays, but it is not byte-identical to retail. The game-code denominator is 1,253,440 bytes, ending at 0x533040.
