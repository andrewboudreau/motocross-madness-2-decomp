The honest headline: 56.3 percent of game-code bytes, 705,475 bytes, are strict exact, about 4 percent of that only in samples. Another 36.4 percent is registered but not exact, 3.4 percent needs inline assembly and is excluded under current rules, 2.5 percent is alignment padding, and 1.4 percent has no registration at all. The project is explicit that its 99.6 percent figure, 2,923 of 2,936 selected targets, is "not 99.6% of MCM2": targets are chosen for usefulness and tractability, and the full function inventory isn't established. No game function is yet a complete original translation unit, and nothing has been linked. The game-code denominator is 1,253,440 bytes, ending at 0x533040.
