18

The team said they invented recording techniques to capture real high-performance motocross engines, and the sound is meant to feel like riding in a pack. In code, RaceSound sorts eleven channels by distance to the camera, clamped at 500, and gives the nearest four full engine voices. Samples are organized as eight sets by three engine classes by ten samples. PCSoundInterface wraps DirectSound with a 3D listener and probes for EAX 1.0; Ctrl+S, Ctrl+C and Ctrl+V toggle sound, music and EAX. CD audio uses MCI. On input, InputDevice has keyboard, mouse and joystick subclasses with PC versions on DirectInput; ControlInterface manages up to eight joysticks, and twelve force-feedback effect GUIDs are named. Menus support up to four GUIUsers routing input devices.
