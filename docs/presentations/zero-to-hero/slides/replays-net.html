VCRInterface is a GameObject that hands recording and playback to a worker thread: eleven auto-reset events, WaitForMultipleObjects, and a 256-slot ring. Replay files can live in memory in 0x19000-byte blocks or on disk via stdio. KrustyVCR files start with "MCMVCR" and version "2.0" plus a Ctrl-Z, followed by eleven racer records, which lines up with up to ten AI opponents plus the player. Networking wraps DirectPlay 4 with the lobby interface; enumeration caps are 5 connections, 5 sessions and 7 players, and there's a 65-case DirectPlay error reporter. NetThread uses _beginthreadex. MSZoneInterface reports scores to the MSN Gaming Zone. The FollowRacer grouping is provisional per the docs.
