Anatomy of a bike, as LoadBike builds it

Rider

A skinned character with 29 pose handles

Handlebars

A moving part with contact points at ±1.305

Rear wheel

Rotating shock, plus a Tire

Front wheel

Inline fork shock, plus a Tire

Gearbox

Gear ratios and RPM limits, validated on load

Emitters

Dust, dirt chunks, steam and sparks

Inertia

Box inertia for bike and rider

Crash sense

Lying on its side means within 5° of flat

EXACT MATCH

The shock pass

Each wheel's spring force is projected onto the ground normal, scaled by the cosine of lean, then split into that wheel's share of load and a moment on the body.

Bike::LoadBike is one of the big loaders, 6,745 bytes, and only partly matched. It sets loadWeight to 165 and a side-lie threshold of cos 5 degrees, builds the rider as a D3DIMSoultreeCharacter, adds the "Handlebars" MovingPart, two wheels with a rotating rear shock and an inline front shock each with a Tire, attaches dust, dirt-chunk and steam emitters, and sets three contact points: the two bar ends at plus and minus 1.305 and one on the model. Vehicle::LoadVehicle adds the gearbox, smoothers, spark emitter, steering control and hit callbacks. The "crash sense" card is my plain-language reading of the sideLieThreshold. The per-wheel shock pass at 0x529450 matches exactly.
