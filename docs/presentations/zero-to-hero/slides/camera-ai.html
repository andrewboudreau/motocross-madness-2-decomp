The camera stack is a six-deep inheritance chain; ShadowCamera also derives from PCCamera for terrain shadows. FollowCamera keeps eased 20-byte values that move toward a target by min(dt, rate)/rate each frame, which gives the smooth chase feel. The docs describe the clamped 10-to-70 value as distance-derived FOV "or similar", so treat the label as provisional. On the AI side, BikeAI.cpp contains landing and jump prediction, obstacle queries, a racing-line builder and a 17,585-byte two-wheel look-ahead simulation. "AI riders simulate their own bike forward" is my plain reading of that name. Separately, CarProcedural drives scene vehicles along Hermite paths with steering clamped to plus or minus 30 degrees; these are likely the moving vehicles 2000-era previews mention, though the docs don't call them traffic.
