Source: RTTI chains in the retail EXE (CLASS_MODEL, PCGAME)

05

RTTI type descriptors give us the inheritance chains directly, so this layering is solid evidence rather than guesswork. The portable layer defines abstract services: Game, RenderTarget, TextureMap, VideoCard, InputDevice, SoundInterface, Camera, ControlInterface. Each has a PC subclass that does the Windows and DirectX work. The game itself, TrackGame, derives from PCGame. A curiosity: DirectDraw is not imported from ddraw.dll at all; DirectDrawCreateEx comes from blade.dll, which identifies itself as the Microsoft Blade Software Rasterizer, and there's a "Blade" renderer GUID alongside HAL, RGB, MMX, Ramp and Ref.
